Hugh is a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Materials Science and Metallurgy and focusses on combining autonomous materials synthesis and characterisation with generative AI methods to discover new materials.
His PhD research in the Inorganic Chemistry Department at the University of Oxford used solid-state synthesis, x-ray diffraction, and density functional theory to investigate disorder and carrier localisation in emerging bismuth-based energy materials.
He completed a BA (Hons) in Nanoscience, Physics, and the Chemistry of Advanced Materials at Trinity College Dublin, and an MSc. in Advanced Materials Science and Engineering at Imperial College London.
