Lili is a PhD student in the Department of Materials Science and Metallurgy, working under the Materials 4.0 CDT. Her research focuses on the automation of perovskite synthesis using spin-coating and machine learning algorithms to help accelerate the discovery and optimisation of clean-energy relevant materials. She completed her integrated Masters in Materials Science at the University of Oxford.
